TheCafeDesk

Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

In short: this app is a private tool that posts TheCafeDesk's own videos to TheCafeDesk's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, it does not collect viewers' data, and nothing is sold.

What this app is

TheCafeDesk makes videos about cafes and places to work. The TheCafeDesk app is an automation that runs on the creator's own computer, using the n8n workflow software. It uploads finished videos, with their titles and captions, to the creator's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.

Who uses it

Only the TheCafeDesk creator signs in to this app. It has no public sign-up, and it does not ask anyone else to connect an account.

Data the app accesses

With the creator's permission, the app uses these services:

  • YouTube (Google): the permissions granted to upload videos to the creator's channel and set their details, such as title, description, tags and privacy status.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): access to the creator's Page and linked Instagram account, in order to publish videos and captions.
  • Sign-in tokens: the access keys these services issue so the app can act for the creator.

The app does not read or collect information about viewers, subscribers, followers or commenters. It does not access Gmail, Google Drive or contacts.

How data is used

Data is used only to publish the creator's videos and record that they were posted. It is not used for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens and app settings stay on the creator's own computer, in n8n's local storage, where n8n encrypts saved credentials. Video files, captions and post records are also stored on that computer. Videos and captions are sent to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram when they are published, and those companies' own policies then apply.

Sharing

Data from Google, YouTube and Meta is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone except those services as needed to publish the videos, or where the law requires it.

Google API Services

This app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.

Keeping and deleting data

Sign-in tokens are kept only while the app is connected. Access can be removed at any time:

  • Google and YouTube: at Google Account third-party connections.
  • Facebook: in Facebook settings, under Apps and Websites.
  • Instagram: in Instagram settings, under Apps and Websites.

Deleting the connection in n8n removes the tokens stored on the computer. If you believe this app holds data about you, email the address below and it will be deleted. Please include enough detail to find it.

This website

This page sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics. The company hosting it may keep standard technical logs.

Children

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.
